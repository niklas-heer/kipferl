## Working title

Kipferl 0.6: Rust-powered Python CLIs, baked smaller

## Opening

Kipferl 0.6 is the first stable release under our new name and the first built
on Rust. It keeps the pleasant Python-style workflow, adds a much stronger
terminal experience, and ships tree-shaken standalone applications from 1.4
MB—with no Python, Rust, libc, or musl installation on the target machine.

## Story

1. **A new name and foundation** — introduce the μcharm/ucharm rename, link the
   migration retrospective, and summarize why stable Rust improves ownership,
   tooling, ecosystem access, and long-term maintenance.
2. **Developer experience** — show `kipferl dev`, direct `tui` and `input`
   imports, Ratatui-backed prompts, and one-command standalone builds.
3. **Useful batteries** — highlight JSON, YAML, TOML, KDL, XML, CSV, INI/CFG,
   SQLite, HTTPS, archives, crypto, filesystem, and process support without
   presenting Kipferl as a CPython or pip replacement.
4. **Tree shaking** — link the technical deep dive and show the measured
   Apple Silicon result: 1,450,837-byte core app, 69.9% smaller than the
   4,817,925-byte full build, with 7.679 ms median startup.
5. **Compatibility** — report 1,669/1,669 available checks, the preserved
   `MCHARM01` format, and the temporary deprecated `ucharm` transition paths.
